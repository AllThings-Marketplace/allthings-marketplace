Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AllThings Escrow, 6-digit delivery PINs, service bookings, payment recovery, and vendor payouts.

How does AllThings Escrow protect my payment?

When you pay for a physical product or fund a service booking via Paystack, your money is not sent directly to the seller. Instead, it is locked safely in AllThings Escrow. The vendor or service professional is paid only after you confirm delivery using your 6-digit Delivery PIN or confirm that your service job is completed.

I was debited by my bank during checkout, but my order still shows "Unpaid". What should I do?

Do not pay twice. Use the 1-Click Instant Payment Recovery tool right here in the Help & Resolution Center (or click the recovery link on your Order Details page). Our system will query Paystack directly using your transaction reference, verify the debit, and immediately update your order status to Escrow Paid.

What is the 6-Digit Delivery Handshake PIN and when should I share it?

Every paid product item generates a unique 6-Digit Delivery PIN visible on your Order Confirmation and Order Details pages. You should give this PIN to the dispatch rider (or enter it on your Order Details page) only after you have inspected your package and confirmed it is correct and undamaged. Sharing or confirming the PIN releases the escrow funds to the vendor.

How are delivery fees and courier options calculated at checkout?

At checkout, our logistics engine calculates live rates across multiple dispatch tiers—including AllThings Express, GIG Logistics, and Inter-State Waybill—based on the vendor's verified dispatch state, your delivery destination across all 36 Nigerian states and Abuja (FCT), and the weight/size class of your items.

Am I charged immediately when I request a Service Professional?

No. Submitting a service booking request carries zero upfront charge. For fixed-rate or package services, you only fund escrow after the professional confirms availability for your preferred date and time. For custom inspection jobs, the professional will send an official itemized Custom Quote (Labor + Materials) for you to review and accept before you fund escrow.

Why can't I share my phone number, WhatsApp, or email in pre-booking inquiries?

To protect both buyers and providers from off-platform fraud, all pre-booking inquiries and order chats are kept strictly inside AllThings. Transactions or payments arranged outside the platform are not covered by AllThings Escrow or dispute resolution.

What happens when I click "Report Issue / Freeze Escrow"?

Clicking Report Issue / Freeze Escrow on an order item or service booking immediately pauses the automated 72-hour vendor payout timer and locks the funds in escrow. An official dispute ticket is opened where both you and the provider can upload photo evidence for the AllThings resolution team to arbitrate.

Which situations qualify for a return or escrow refund?

You are eligible for a full refund or replacement if your item arrives damaged or defective, the wrong product or variant is delivered, items are missing, the item materially differs from its listing description, the vendor fails to dispatch your order, or a service professional fails to honour an accepted booking. Read our full Returns & Refunds Policy for complete details.

How do I become a verified Vendor or Service Provider on AllThings?

Click Become a Vendor or Provider and complete our 5-stage onboarding wizard: (1) Email & SMS OTP verification, (2) Government ID & live selfie KYC, (3) Business profile & optional CAC credentials, (4) Store/base address & GPS capture, and (5) Real-time Paystack NUBAN bank account name-match verification.

When can vendors withdraw their earnings to their Nigerian bank account?

As soon as a buyer confirms delivery via their 6-digit PIN (or confirms service completion, or 72 hours pass after completion with no dispute), your net earnings (90% of item/service value) move from Locked in Escrow to your Available for Withdrawal wallet balance. You can then trigger an instant Paystack bank transfer to your verified NUBAN account from your Provider Dashboard.
