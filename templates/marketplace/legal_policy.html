{% if active_slug == 'privacy-policy' %}

1. Purpose and Scope

AllThings Digital Marketplace ("AllThings") respects the privacy of its customers, retail vendors, service providers, employees, and other individuals whose personal information it processes. This Privacy Policy explains what information the platform collects, why it collects it, how it is used, when it is shared, how it is protected, and how individuals may exercise their data protection rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and applicable regulations.

2. Categories of Personal Information Collected

AllThings may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Account Information: Legal names, email addresses, Nigerian telephone numbers, usernames, encrypted account credentials, dates of birth, and profile details.
  • Vendor & Service Provider KYC Information: Registered business names, business contact details, government-issued identification documents (NIN, Driver's License, International Passport, or Voter's Card), live verification selfies, CAC registration certificates, shop/studio verification photos, and settlement bank account details (NUBAN) required for approved payouts.
  • Transaction Information: Physical product orders, service bookings, custom quotes, invoices, Paystack payment references, escrow status, refunds, dispute tickets, and transaction ledger histories.
  • Location Information: Customer delivery addresses, landmarks, service site locations, vendor dispatch states/regions, and GPS coordinates where authorised for courier rate estimation or store verification.
  • Technical & Usage Information: IP addresses, device and browser metadata, security logs, cookies, and platform interaction records.
  • Communication Information: Pre-booking inquiries, order chat transcripts, customer support tickets, verified product/service reviews, ratings, and dispute evidence.
Payment Card Security: Payment card credentials and bank authorization flows are handled directly through approved payment processors (such as Paystack). AllThings does not store raw credit or debit card numbers on its servers.

3. Purposes of Processing & Lawful Basis

We process personal information under documented lawful bases (contractual necessity, legal obligation, legitimate business interests, and informed consent) to:

  • Create, verify, and manage customer, vendor, and service provider accounts.
  • Connect customers with relevant verified vendors and service professionals across Nigeria.
  • Process orders, service bookings, escrow locks, delivery PIN handshakes, payouts, and refunds.
  • Coordinate parcel and cargo dispatch with integrated logistics partners.
  • Perform KYC identity verification, prevent off-platform fraud, and arbitrate escrow disputes.
  • Improve marketplace search, category recommendations, and platform security.
  • Meet applicable Nigerian legal, regulatory, tax, and financial accounting obligations.
  • Send transactional alerts (OTP codes, order updates, escrow notices) and, where legally permitted with opt-out controls, promotional communications.

4. Location Privacy

Location data is used to display nearby vendors, identify service coverage radii, calculate multi-courier shipping fees, and verify physical vendor operating bases. Where technically feasible, AllThings provides users with a choice between manual address selection and device-based GPS capture. Continuous background location tracking is never performed, and users may revoke device-level location permissions at any time through their browser or mobile operating system settings.

5. Information Sharing & Third-Party Safeguards

AllThings does not sell personal information. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Vendors & Service Providers: Only the order or booking details necessary to prepare items for courier pickup or perform an accepted service contract. Direct customer email addresses are never exposed to vendors.
  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Delivery recipient names, phone numbers, and destination addresses required by dispatch riders (such as AllThings Express, GIG Logistics, or inter-state waybill operators) to complete delivery.
  • Payment & Verification Gateways: Paystack (for payment collection, NUBAN resolution, and vendor transfers), Brevo (transactional email delivery), Termii (SMS OTP verification), and Cloudinary (secure media optimization).
  • Public Authorities & Auditors: Professional advisers or competent Nigerian regulatory and law-enforcement authorities where disclosure is required by law.

6. Data Security, Retention & Your NDPA Rights

AllThings implements technical and organisational safeguards including HTTPS encryption, CSRF protection, row-level database locking on financial ledgers, OTP verification, and role-based access controls. Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with Nigerian statutory retention periods.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), you have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate records, deletion where legally available, or restriction/withdrawal of consent for optional processing. Submit requests via the Help & Resolution Center or email support@allthings.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

1. What Are Cookies & Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit AllThings. Alongside related browser storage technologies such as localStorage, cookies help the platform recognize your session, keep your shopping cart intact, protect forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF), and remember your preferences in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID).

2. How AllThings Categorizes Cookies

Category Technical Name / Storage Purpose on AllThings Duration & Consent
Strictly Necessary sessionid, csrftoken, allthings_cookie_consent Maintains login sessions, shopping cart items, CSRF form security, escrow checkout tokens, and your saved cookie consent choice. Session to 1 year (Always Active)
Functional localStorage.recentSearches Remembers your last 8 search terms in your browser so you can quickly re-run recent searches from the top search bar. Persistent in browser until cleared (Requires Consent)
Analytics & Personalization First-party interaction logs (UserInteraction) Powers the "Continue Where You Left Off" and "Picked For You" homepage shelves and helps us understand which departments are most active. Up to 180 days (Requires Consent)
Marketing Campaign attribution parameters Measures promotional banner effectiveness and external marketing campaigns when enabled. Up to 180 days (Requires Consent)

3. How to Manage or Withdraw Your Consent

When you visit AllThings for the first time, our Cookie Consent Banner lets you Accept All, Reject Optional, or Customize your preferences. Turning off Functional cookies immediately clears and pauses `recentSearches` in your browser, and turning off Analytics cookies switches your homepage to non-personalized trending shelves.

5. Account Suspension, Liability & Governing Law

{% elif active_slug == 'provider-agreement' %}

1. Eligibility, 5-Stage KYC Verification & Approval

Every individual or business seeking to sell physical products or offer professional services on AllThings must complete our multi-stage onboarding and KYC verification workflow:

  • Stage 1 (Contact Verification): Verified legal name, official email OTP, and Nigerian phone SMS OTP.
  • Stage 2 (Identity KYC): Valid government-issued ID (NIN, Driver's License, International Passport, or PVC) and a clear live selfie.
  • Stage 3 (Business Credentials): Department specialty, bio, and CAC registration documents where applicable.
  • Stage 4 (Location & GPS Pinning): Physical shop, studio, or operating base address in Nigeria, GPS coordinates, and interior/exterior workspace photos.
  • Stage 5 (Paystack NUBAN Settlement): A 10-digit Nigerian bank account whose resolved account name matches the verified legal name or registered business name.

Verification badges confirm only the specific identity and location checks completed by AllThings and do not exempt providers from ongoing quality and fulfilment standards.

2. Listing Standards & Content Safety

Vendors must upload genuine photos, accurate stock counts, accurate package weights/shipping tiers, and honest condition disclosures (New, Refurbished, or Used). Service providers must clearly define their pricing model (Fixed, Starting, Hourly, Tiered Packages, or Custom Quote), service mode, travel radius, and what is included or excluded. Listings containing phone numbers, external URLs, WhatsApp numbers, or bank details are automatically flagged by our anti-leakage filter.

3. Order Fulfilment, Service Honouring & Commissions

  • Product Vendors: Must promptly accept paid store orders, package items securely, and mark them Ready for Pickup so a courier rider can be dispatched using the 6-digit pickup PIN.
  • Service Professionals: Must honour accepted booking schedules or custom quotes professionally and mark jobs completed only when the agreed scope of work has been delivered.
  • Platform Commission & Escrow Payouts: AllThings deducts the disclosed platform commission (default 10% commission; 90% net vendor earnings) upon escrow release. Cleared earnings move from your Locked in Escrow balance to your Available for Withdrawal wallet balance upon customer PIN confirmation, service confirmation, or expiration of the 72-hour post-completion window (unless paused by an active customer dispute).
{% elif active_slug == 'returns-and-refunds' %}

1. General Principles & Customer Cancellations

Returns, cancellations, and refunds are governed by the stage of order fulfilment, the disclosed condition of the product, the service booking terms, and Nigerian consumer protection law. Customers or vendors may cancel an order item before dispatch, which automatically reverses the pending escrow allocation and restores product stock.

2. Eligible Return & Escrow Refund Situations

A customer may open a formal dispute and request a return or escrow refund where:

  • The delivered product arrives damaged, defective, or non-functional.
  • The wrong product, model, or variant was delivered, or an item is missing from the package.
  • The product materially differs from its listing description or disclosed condition, or is counterfeit.
  • The vendor fails to dispatch the order within the required fulfilment window.
  • A service professional fails to attend an accepted booking or materially fails to deliver the agreed scope of work.
  • A duplicate debit or payment reconciliation error occurred during checkout.

3. How the 72-Hour Escrow Freeze & Arbitration Process Works

  1. Do Not Share Your Delivery PIN Prematurely: For physical goods, never share your 6-digit Delivery Handshake PIN with the dispatch rider until you have inspected the package.
  2. Freeze Escrow via Report Issue: Clicking Report Issue / Freeze Escrow on your Order Details or Bookings page immediately pauses the 72-hour automated vendor payout timer and opens an official Support Ticket.
  3. Evidence & Investigation: Both the customer and the provider can upload photographic evidence and notes in the Support & Resolution Center. AllThings administrators review the order ledger, courier tracking, and chat logs to issue a fair resolution (full refund, partial adjustment, or escrow release).
{% elif active_slug == 'shipping-and-delivery' %}

1. Multi-Courier Logistics & Rate Calculation

AllThings integrates a multi-tier logistics engine (including AllThings Express Bike, GIG Logistics, and Inter-State Waybill / Cargo Van) to deliver products across all 36 Nigerian states and Abuja (FCT). Delivery fees are dynamically calculated at checkout based on the vendor's verified dispatch region, the buyer's destination state/city, package weight, and shipping class (Small Parcel, Medium Box, or Heavy/Bulky Cargo).

2. 6-Digit Pickup & Delivery Handshake PINs

  • Vendor Pickup Handshake: When a vendor marks an accepted order item Ready for Pickup, the system generates a 6-digit Pickup PIN and assigns a courier rider.
  • Customer Delivery Handshake: Upon payment, the buyer receives a unique 6-digit Delivery PIN for each ordered item. Providing this PIN to the rider (or confirming it on your Order Details page) serves as cryptographic proof of delivery and releases the item's escrow funds to the vendor.

3. Failed, Delayed, or Damaged Deliveries

Vendors must prepare and hand over accepted orders promptly. If a delivery attempt fails due to an unreachable phone number, restricted site access, or an incomplete address, support will coordinate with the buyer and courier to reschedule. If a package arrives visibly damaged or incorrect, do not release your 6-digit Delivery PIN—immediately open a dispute in the Resolution Center with photos of the package.

{% elif active_slug == 'payments-and-payouts' %}

1. Authorised Payment Channels & Verification

All customer payments on AllThings are processed in Nigerian Naira (NGN) through Paystack (supporting debit cards, bank transfers, and USSD). An order or service booking is marked as PAID only when AllThings receives cryptographic webhook confirmation or server-to-server verification from Paystack. Self-reported payment screenshots are never accepted as proof of payment.If your bank account is debited during a network drop, our 1-Click Instant Payment Recovery tool in the Support Center reconciles your reference directly with Paystack.

2. How AllThings Escrow Protection Works

When a buyer pays for a product order or funds a service booking, the net earnings are credited to the provider's Locked in Escrow (`pending_balance`) ledger. Funds move to the provider's Available for Withdrawal (`available_balance`) wallet only when:

  • The customer confirms delivery using their 6-digit Delivery PIN (for physical products), or
  • The customer clicks Confirm Job on a completed service booking, or
  • 72 hours have elapsed since the order/service was marked completed without the customer opening a dispute ticket.

3. Vendor Payout Schedule & Security Controls

Active verified providers can withdraw cleared funds (minimum NGN 100.00) directly to their verified Nigerian bank account via Paystack Transfers. To protect marketplace liquidity and prevent unauthorized account takeovers, withdrawals are subject to a 7-day payout cooldown window, row-level database locking, and password + KYC name-match verification whenever a payout bank account is updated.
